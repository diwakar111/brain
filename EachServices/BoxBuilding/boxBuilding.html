Our Process

From BOM to Box — Our 6-Step Build Process

A repeatable, documented manufacturing flow with full traceability at every stage — from the first purchase order to the final pallet.

01

Requirements & BOM Review

We review your assembly drawings, schematics, and bill of materials. Our engineers flag any component availability risks, suggest alternatives, and produce a detailed cost-and-lead-time breakdown before any commitment.

Days 1–3
02

Component Sourcing & Procurement

We source all components from authorised distributors — Mouser, Arrow, Digi-Key, and direct manufacturer channels — ensuring authentic parts with full traceability. Incoming goods inspection is performed on arrival.

Week 1–2
03

PCB Assembly & Sub-Build

SMT placement, reflow soldering, through-hole wave soldering, and manual rework where required. AOI (Automated Optical Inspection) and X-ray inspection for BGA components. IPC-A-610 Class 2/3 standards.

Week 2–3
04

Mechanical Integration & Wiring

PCBs, power supplies, displays, and mechanical sub-assemblies are fitted into the enclosure per build instructions. Wiring harnesses are routed, terminated, and dressed. Torque specifications recorded for all fasteners.

Week 3–4
05

Burn-In & Functional Testing

Every unit is powered for a defined burn-in period (typically 48–72 hrs) to catch infant mortality failures. Automated functional test fixtures verify all I/O, communications, and power rails against your test spec.

Week 4–5
06

Quality Inspection & Shipment

Final cosmetic and dimensional inspection. Serial number assignment, label printing, certificate of conformance generation. Anti-static packaging, customs documentation, and door-to-door logistics management.

Week 5–6

Standards, Tools & Technologies We Use

Every build follows documented processes backed by industry standards and verified with specialised inspection equipment.

IPC Standards

IPC-A-610, IPC-J-STD-001, IPC-7711/7721 rework and repair. Class 2 and Class 3 build capability.

AOI & X-Ray Inspection

Automated Optical Inspection post-reflow. X-ray for BGA/CSP solder joint validation. 3D AOI available.

BOM Management

Multi-level BOM parsing, approved vendor list management, lifecycle tracking, and alternative part qualification.

Conformal Coating

Acrylic, urethane and silicone coatings applied by selective spray or dip. IPC-CC-830 compliant thickness.

ESD Control

ANSI/ESD S20.20 compliant workspace. Ionisers, grounded mats, wrist straps, and ESD-safe packaging throughout.

IP-Rated Enclosures

IP54 to IP67 rated assemblies. Gasket cutting, potting, and pressure-decay leak testing for harsh environments.

Batch Production

Prototype (1–10 units) through medium volume (up to 5,000 units/month). Full serialisation and traceability.

Firmware Flashing

In-line firmware programming via JTAG, SWD, UART or USB. Golden image verification and version stamping per unit.
