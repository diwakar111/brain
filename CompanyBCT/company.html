Our Story

Built on Expertise & Reliability

BrainChild Technology was founded in 2007 with a focused mission — to create innovative embedded products and deliver them with unmatched expertise and reliability. What started as a small engineering team in Bangalore has grown into a full-service technology partner trusted by product companies, automotive suppliers, and industrial manufacturers worldwide.

We believe the best technology is built close to the problem it solves. Every firmware line, every PCB trace, every IoT network we design is engineered with real-world operating conditions in mind — because our clients depend on it working perfectly, every time.
